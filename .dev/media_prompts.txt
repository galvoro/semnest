SEMNEST — prompts for the generated media (theme: a dinosaur nest in a lost
valley; adventure, but quiet).

Where the files go
  Opening image  -> save the two PNGs as .dev/media/title-wide.png and
                    .dev/media/title-mobile.png, then run  sh .dev/make_title.sh
                    (makes assets/title-wide.webp and assets/title-mobile.webp;
                    the game then shows them instead of the placeholder).
                    .dev/media/ is not committed.
  Music          -> save Suno's MP3 as .dev/media/bgm.mp3; it then goes to
                    assets/bgm.mp3 with loop points that skip the intro and
                    the fade-out (BGM_FILE and BGM_LOOP in audio.js).

  sandstone paper #EFEADB, basalt ink #25261F, lagoon teal #247C8C (model A),
  amber #C47718 (model B), fern green #4A8236, earth brown #6E5638,
  jungle greens #8DAA8E / #5F8462 / #3F6045, morning mist #BFD9D4 -> #F3E6C6.

1. BACKGROUND MUSIC (Suno)
=====================================================================
In Suno: Create > Custom.
  - Instrumental: on.
  - Model: the newest one.
  - Title: SEMNEST
  - Styles: paste block A.
  - Exclude styles (under More options): paste block B.
  - If offered: Weirdness about 30 %, Style influence about 70 %.
  - Lyrics box: leave empty, or paste block C (tags only, no words) to steer
    the form. If Instrumental hides the lyrics box, skip C.
Every run gives two versions; make a few runs and pick the one with the most
even groove and the shortest intro and ending. Suno usually adds a short
intro and a fade-out and makes tracks of 2 to 4 minutes; that is fine. Save
the MP3 as .dev/media/bgm.mp3 and tell me: I set the loop points in
audio.js (skipping the intro and the fade-out, with a crossfade at the
seam), copy it to assets/bgm.mp3 and switch the music on.

--- a. landscape ---------------------------------------------------

Landscape 3:2 key art for the opening screen of an educational game called
"SEMNEST" (about structural equation models, and which model is nested in
which).

TITLE
In the upper left third, over the sky, render the title "SEMNEST" in capital
letters, clearly and legibly, in a bold, high-contrast vintage serif (like
the cover of an old expedition book; similar to Fraunces Black). Colour
#25261F. Spelling must be exact: S-E-M-N-E-S-T, one word, no hyphen, no extra
letters. Directly below it, much smaller, in an italic serif of the same
family: "nested · equivalent · neither".

SUBJECT
A hidden, lost-world valley at first light. In the middle ground, slightly
right of centre, a large dinosaur ground nest on a sandy clearing: a wide,
low ring-shaped mound of earth, lined with fern fronds and moss. Nested
inside it is a second, smaller ring-shaped nest (the "nest within a nest").
In the small inner nest lie two large, elongated, speckled eggs, one lagoon
teal and one amber; a cream-coloured speckled egg lies in the outer nest.
At the right edge, partly hidden behind tree ferns and partly cropped by the
frame, a calm duck-billed dinosaur (a hadrosaur such as Maiasaura, the "good
mother lizard") stands guard and looks down at the nest: gentle and
dignified, mouth closed, not menacing. Far away in the haze, two small
silhouettes of long-necked sauropods.
On a flat rock in the lower left middle ground lies an explorer's open field
notebook with a brass compass next to it; on the page is a simple pencil
sketch of a path diagram: two circles joined by a curved double-headed
arrow, with straight arrows from the circles down to six small squares. No
writing on the page.

SETTING
Behind the valley rise misty, flat-topped sandstone cliffs (tepuis) with one
thin waterfall. Tree ferns, cycads and dense jungle frame the scene. Soft,
warm morning light from the upper right, light mist in the valley, a gentle
sky from pale mist-teal to warm cream.

COMPOSITION
Keep the bottom fifth calm and fairly dark (ferns and ground in shadow, no
important details), because the PLAY and LEARN buttons sit there. Keep the
title, the nest and the notebook away from the top and bottom 8 % (the image
is cropped a little on wide screens).

STYLE
Painterly editorial illustration with the feel of a vintage expedition book
or museum poster: clean shapes, soft shadows, gentle haze, subtle paper
grain. Adventurous but quiet; professional, suitable for university
teaching. NOT photographic, NOT 3D-rendered, NOT cartoonish or childish, no
gore, no bared teeth, no people, no birds.

PALETTE
Mist-teal sky #BFD9D4 to warm cream #F3E6C6; jungle greens #8DAA8E, #5F8462,
#3F6045; sandstone #EFE9DA; earth brown #6E5638; egg accents lagoon teal
#247C8C and amber #C47718; title #25261F.

CONSTRAINTS
The ONLY text in the image is the title and the line below it. No other
words, captions, numbers, logos, signatures, watermarks or frames.

OUTPUT
Landscape, aspect ratio 3:2 (1536 x 1024).

--- b. portrait (same chat, after a.) --------------------------------

Now make a portrait 2:3 version (1024 x 1536) of the same image for phones:
the same world, style, palette and egg colours, with the elements stacked
vertically. The title "SEMNEST" (exact spelling) and the line "nested ·
equivalent · neither" at the top, centred. The cliffs with the waterfall
behind, the nest with the nested inner nest and the three eggs in the middle,
the hadrosaur parent half behind ferns at one side, the field notebook with
the sketched path diagram on a rock just below the nest. Keep the title and
the nest within the middle 60 % of the width (phones crop the left and right
edges) and keep the bottom quarter calm and dark for the buttons. The only
